## Problem

The audit proves only a one-player loop. Multiplayer pickup, cart-to-Style transfer, placement, voting, and room reveals still lack evidence.

## Acceptance

- Two or more players can collect items without cross-player ownership errors.
- Every player receives the correct owned collection in Style.
- Players can place, rotate, recolor, and retrieve items in their own rooms.
- Players cannot place items in another player's room.
- Players cannot vote for their own room.
- Vote submissions appear in the correct reveal payload and leaderboard.
